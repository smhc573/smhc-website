Privacy and cookies

Your information.How this website handles it.

What happens to information when you use this website, including our referral and careers enquiry forms.

Who we are

This website is run by St Matthews Ltd, trading as St Matthews Healthcare, registered in England, company number 4611507. Our registered office is 29-31 St Matthews Parade, Northampton NN2 7HF.

St Matthews Ltd is responsible, as the data controller, for information you send us through this website.

What this website collects on its own

Nothing about you. This website does not use cookies, visitor statistics or any kind of tracking, and it does not load fonts, maps or videos from other companies.

The one exception is the official ratings widget from the Care Quality Commission on each service page, which the law asks us to show. It loads from the CQC website, and the CQC’s own privacy notice covers it.

Like every website, the company that hosts this one may keep standard technical records, such as the address of the device that asked for a page, to keep the site secure and running.

Your text size setting

If you change the text size, your browser remembers your choice on your own device so the site opens at the same size next time. It is never sent to us. Clearing your browser history removes it.

Our referral and careers forms

Our forms do not send or store anything on the website. When you press Send referral or Send enquiry, the form opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. Nothing reaches us unless you send that email yourself.

Referrals go to our referral team at smhc.referrals@nhs.net. Careers enquiries go to our recruitment team at recruitment@smhc.uk.com. We use what you send to respond to your referral or enquiry, and to arrange an assessment, a visit or an interview if you would like to go ahead.

Please do not include the name, date of birth or NHS number of the person you are referring. We will ask for those securely when we speak.

Why we can use your details

We use the details in your email because we have a legitimate interest in responding to enquiries about our services and about working with us. For careers enquiries, we also use them to take the steps you ask us to take before any offer of employment.

Who we share them with

We do not sell your information or share it for marketing. We only share it where we need to in order to deal with your referral or enquiry, or where the law requires us to.

How long we keep them

We keep enquiry emails only for as long as we need them to deal with your referral or enquiry, in line with our records retention policy.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it, limit how we use it or stop using it. To make a request, write to Data protection, St Matthews Ltd, 29-31 St Matthews Parade, Northampton NN2 7HF.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies of its own, so there is no cookie banner. If that changes, for example if we add visitor statistics, we will update this notice and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
