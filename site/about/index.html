Our vision, in the words of our Chief Executive

Our vision is a world where every person, regardless of the complexity of their needs, has the opportunity to live with dignity, purpose and hope. We strive to be the very best provider of specialist care, trusted by families, commissioners and communities to support people when others cannot.

By putting people first, living our values every day, and creating a culture where everyone is heard and valued, we will deliver outstanding care that changes lives, builds confidence, and helps people achieve their fullest potential.

At St Matthews, we see the person before the condition, the potential before the limitation, and the future before the challenge. We aspire to be recognised as the most trusted and respected provider of specialist care, transforming lives through exceptional support and never losing sight of what matters most: the person in front of us.
