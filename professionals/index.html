How a referral works

From the first call to the admission.

Timescales are agreed per referral. Whatever the route in, the person is assessed by our clinical team before admission.

  1. 1
    Step 1The enquiryOne referral line and one inbox reach every service in the group.
  2. 2
    Step 2The assessmentPre admission assessment by our clinical team, wherever the person is, with a clear view of what we can offer.
  3. 3
    Step 3The matchThe service, the unit, single sex where it matters, and the observation level.
  4. 4
    Step 4The admissionDischarge to assess, planned from hospital or home, respite, and Section 117.

Legal frameworks

What each service states

  • The Dallingtons: Detained under the Mental Health Act 1983, or informal; Ministry of Justice restricted patients; Section 117 aftercare and CPA
  • St Matthews Hospital: Detained under the Mental Health Act
  • Willowbrook House: Conditional discharge from the Ministry of Justice; Community treatment order; Guardianship

Other services do not state legal frameworks in their published material. Confirm at referral.

Funding

Agreed with the commissioner

Placements are funded through the NHS and local authorities, including Section 117 aftercare. Observation levels and the cost of a placement are agreed per referral. Fees are a conversation, never a published price.
