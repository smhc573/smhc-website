The St Matthews pathway

Eight stages. One provider, the whole way.

From the first call about somebody nobody else will take, to a home they do not have to leave again. The same provider at every stage, so the placement search never has to start over.

  1. 01
    The enquiryOne referral line and one inbox reach every service in the group.
  2. 02
    The assessmentAssessed by our clinical team before admission, wherever the person is.
  3. 03
    The matchThe service, the unit, single sex where it matters, and the observation level.
  4. 04
    The admissionDischarge to assess, planned from hospital or home, respite, and Section 117.
  5. 05
    Settling inA room they can truly call home, with a personalised care plan reviewed and adapted as often as their needs change.
  6. 06
    Living wellTherapy, activity and outings. The things that make a day worth having.
  7. 07
    Progress and reviewIndependence handed back in stages, against the goals set at the start.
  8. 08
    Moving on, or stayingStep down within our own services, or a home here for as long as needed.

More than a bed, we provide a complete care pathway. From the level of support needed today through to step down within our own services, we offer continuity of care without the need to restart the placement search.
